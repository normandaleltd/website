Normandale has built on its experience to offer a high-quality, high-volume aircraft interior painting facility.

We can offer a wide range of finishes that conform to the high standards required for the aviation and aerospace industries.

Normandale's QMS system is ISO 9001 registered, enabling us to work with any company requiring this standard.
