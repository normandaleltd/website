Established for over 40 years, Normandale is one of the most reputable and largest motorsport paint finishing companies in the UK.

Normandale offers a 24-hour, 7-day-a-week service including collection and delivery. Our facility includes 3 state-of-the-art USI low bake spray booths, a bracketed Spanesi body alignment jig system, and a team of 30 employees with a wide range of experience. This enables us to meet the demands of the modern-day motorsport industry.

Normandale has worked, and is currently working, with major F1 teams as well as numerous single-seater, rally, and race car teams, from small independents through to manufacturer-backed teams.
